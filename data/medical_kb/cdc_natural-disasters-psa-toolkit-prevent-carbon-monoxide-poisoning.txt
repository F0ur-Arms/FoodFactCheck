Prevent Carbon Monoxide (CO) Poisoning
Power outages can occur during emergencies (like hurricanes).
Using gas-powered generators can cause CO to build up in home–poisoning the people inside.
DANGER
Carbon Monoxide
Can't be seen
Can't be smelled
Can't be heard
But–CO poisoning
CO DETECTOR
Can be stopped
Prevent CO Poisoning:
Install a battery-operated CO detector in your home.
Use generators and any gas-powered equipment outside, at least 20 feet away from doors, windows, and vents
