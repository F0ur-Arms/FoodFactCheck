Overview
Essentials for Parenting Toddlers and Preschoolers is a free, online resource for parents and caregivers of children between the ages of two and four years. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) reviewed extensive research to identify the best approaches to parenting toddlers and preschoolers. CDC also talked to the experts—both recognized leaders in the parenting field and parents themselves—to recommend approaches and resources that parents and caregivers can use to build positive, healthy relationships with their children during this challenging and exciting time.
Topics covered
Parenting is hard work! It can also be fun and rewarding. We know that every child and every parent is unique. You face many different situations and challenges every day. We don't take a one-size-fits-all approach or consider this a set of strict parenting rules that must be followed. We hope to give you new ideas for parenting so you can make the best decisions about what can work with your child and family. Topics covered in Essentials for Parenting Toddlers and Preschoolers include:
Tips for Understanding and Enjoying the Early Years
Understand toddler growth, behavior, and how to adjust expectations and support development.
Tips for Noticing and Naming Emotions
Learn to help toddlers manage emotions with calm support and simple emotion coaching steps.
Tips for Connecting and Communicating
Connect with young children through clear communication, active listening, and play.
Tips for Relying on Routines and Rules
Learn how routines and rules help your child know what to expect and can make the days smoother.
Tips for Responding to Behavior
Learn to how to best respond to children's behavior using positive attention and consequences.
An evidence-informed methodology
CDC has funded research and provided expert guidance to protect children, support families, and build nurturing and healthy communities for over 30 years. That is why professionals serving children and their families (including educators, clinicians, clergy, counselors, and therapists) can feel confident recommending CDC resources to the people they serve.
Essentials for Parenting Toddlers and Preschoolers is based on decades of research and practical parenting experience suggesting that certain skills are useful in building a positive relationship and in handling children's challenging behaviors.
Using literature reviews and subject matter expert interviews, CDC developed materials and tested them with focus groups of parents and caregivers across the country with experience raising toddlers and preschoolers. The result is a thoroughly researched and tested collection of easy-to-read articles, instructional videos with real-world examples, and engaging activities that parents and caregivers can use anytime.
