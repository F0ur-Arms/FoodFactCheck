Cancer Risk Factors
Key points
A risk factor is something that increases the chance of developing a disease.
Some examples of risk factors for cancer are alcohol, family health history, human papillomavirus (HPV), obesity, and tobacco.
Risk factors
Alcohol and Cancer
You can lower your risk for cancer by drinking less alcohol or not drinking at all.
Family Health History and Cancer
Learn your family’s history of cancer and share it with your doctor.

HPV and Cancer
Human papillomavirus (HPV) is a common virus that can cause cancer.
Sept. 4, 2024
Obesity and Cancer
Being overweight or having obesity increases your risk of getting cancer.

Tobacco and Cancer
Tobacco use can cause cancer almost anywhere in your body.
