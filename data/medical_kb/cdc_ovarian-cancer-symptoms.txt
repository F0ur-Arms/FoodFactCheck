Symptoms
Ovarian cancer may cause the following signs and symptoms:
Vaginal bleeding (particularly if you are past menopause), or discharge from your vagina that is not normal for you.
Pain or pressure in the pelvic area.
Abdominal or back pain.
Bloating.
Feeling full too quickly, or difficulty eating.
A change in your bathroom habits, such as more frequent or urgent need to urinate and/or constipation.
Pay attention to your body, and know what is normal for you. If you have unusual vaginal bleeding, see a doctor right away. If you have any of the other signs for 2 weeks or longer and they are not normal for you, see a doctor. They may be caused by something other than cancer, but the only way to know is to see a doctor.
