Key points
Different people have different symptoms for prostate cancer.
Most men do not have symptoms at all.
If you have signs or symptoms, see your doctor.
Possible signs and symptoms of prostate cancer
Most prostate cancers do not cause symptoms early on. If you have any of the following symptoms, be sure to see your doctor right away:
Difficulty starting urination.
Weak or interrupted flow of urine.
Urinating often, especially at night.
Trouble emptying the bladder completely.
Pain or burning during urination.
Blood in the urine or semen.
Pain in the back, hips, or pelvis that doesn't go away.
Painful ejaculation.
Keep in mind that these symptoms may be caused by prostate changes or conditions that are not cancer, such as an enlarged prostate.
Your doctor may recommend that you get screened for prostate cancer. Before being screened, learn about the possible benefits and risks of screening.
