Key points
Watery diarrhea is the most common symptom of cyclosporiasis.
Cyclosporiasis symptoms can be different from person to person.
Symptoms usually begin about one week after becoming infected.
See a healthcare provider if you are experiencing symptoms of cyclosporiasis.
Signs and symptoms
Contact your healthcare provider if you have symptoms.
Although most healthy people with cyclosporiasis recover on their own, symptoms may be more severe and last longer without treatment. If you have symptoms, stay well-hydrated.
Symptoms of cyclosporiasis usually begin about one week after becoming infected with the Cyclospora parasite (ranging from 2 days to 2 weeks or more).
Cyclosporiasis is not usually life-threatening, but it can make some people very sick. Children, people with weakened immune systems, and older people may experience more severe illness or complications from severe dehydration.
Without treatment, symptoms can last anywhere from a few days to a month or longer. Some symptoms like diarrhea may go away and then return. Other symptoms like fatigue may continue after gastrointestinal symptoms go away.
Common symptoms
Watery diarrhea
Loss of appetite
Weight loss
Cramping
Bloating
Increased gas
Nausea
Fatigue
Less common symptoms
Vomiting
Body aches
Headache
Low-grade fever
