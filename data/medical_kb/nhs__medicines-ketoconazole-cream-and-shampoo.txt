Ketoconazole cream and shampoo
- Common brands: Daktarin Gold, Daktarin Intensiv, Dandrazol, Nizoral
Ketoconazole is a medicine used to treat and prevent fungal skin infections. You can buy it from shops and pharmacies, but some types are only available with a prescription.
What ketoconazole cream and shampoo are for
Ketoconazole is an antifungal medicine. It's used to treat fungal skin infections and also help stop them coming back if you keep getting them.
Ketoconazole shampoo can be used to treat:
dandruff
seborrhoeic dermatitis, which often causes itchy or flaky skin around your scalp, ears, eyebrows, nose or chest
pityriasis versicolor (tinea versicolor)
Ketoconazole cream can be used to treat:
athlete's foot
ringworm
jock itch (around the groin, top of the legs and buttocks)
thrush on the skin around the vagina (vulva)
How to use ketoconazole cream and shampoo and how much to use (dose)
Ketoconazole usually comes as a cream you put on your skin, or as shampoo.
Which type you need and how long you need to use it for depends on why you're using it.
Always check the packet or leaflet that comes with your medicine. Ask a pharmacist or doctor for advice if you're not sure how to use it or have any problems using it.
How to use common types of ketoconazole
Cream
You'll usually need to apply ketoconazole cream to the affected area once or twice a day for a few weeks, and for a few days after your symptoms have gone.
Speak to a pharmacist or doctor if you're not sure how often or how long to use it for.
When using ketoconazole cream:
make sure the area is clean and dry
apply the cream to the area and gently rub it into your skin – check the packet or leaflet to see how much to use
avoid getting it in your eyes or mouth
wash your hands after using it (unless the infection is on your hands)
Shampoo
When you first start using ketoconazole shampoo for dandruff, you'll usually need to use it every 3 or 4 days for 2 to 4 weeks.
After this, use it once every 1 to 2 weeks to help stop dandruff from coming back.
Speak to a pharmacist or doctor if you're not sure how often or how long to use it for.
When using ketoconazole shampoo for dandruff:
wet your hair and scalp
use enough shampoo to cover your scalp, gently rubbing it to make a foam (lather)
leave this foam for 3 to 5 minutes before rinsing off
avoid getting it in your eyes or mouth
if you're using a steroid lotion or cream to help with dandruff, you may need to gradually use less of this when you start using ketoconazole shampoo – speak to a pharmacist or doctor for advice, or check the instructions that come with the shampoo
If you've been prescribed ketoconazole shampoo for pityriasis versicolor or an infection somewhere else on your skin, your doctor or pharmacist will tell you when and how to use it.
Use ketoconazole cream or shampoo for as long as the instructions, or your pharmacist or doctor, tell you.
If you do not use it for long enough, it may not work or the infection may come back.
If you're using ketoconazole cream, speak to a pharmacist or doctor if your symptoms have got worse or have not started to get better after 2 weeks.
If you miss a dose of ketoconazole
If you forget to use the cream or shampoo, use it as soon as you remember, unless it's nearly time for the next use.
If it's nearly time for the next use, skip the dose you missed and carry on using it at the usual time.
Do not use 2 doses to make up for a missed dose.
If you use too much ketoconazole
Do not worry if you accidentally use more ketoconazole cream or shampoo than you're supposed to. It's unlikely to cause problems. But try to avoid regularly using too much as it can increase the chance of side effects.
Non-urgent advice: Speak to a pharmacist if:
you've used more ketoconazole than it says on the packet, leaflet or prescription and have any symptoms
you've swallowed ketoconazole cream or shampoo and have any symptoms

Side effects of ketoconazole cream and shampoo
Like all medicines, ketoconazole can cause side effects, although not everyone gets them.
Possible side effects of ketoconazole cream and shampoo include:
itchy, irritated or flaking skin
sore or red skin (this may be harder to see on black or brown skin)
pain or a burning feeling on your skin
watering eyes, or hair loss or a change to the texture of your hair, if you're using the shampoo
Ketoconazole cream and shampoo will not usually affect your ability to drive, ride a bike or use machinery.
Non-urgent advice: Speak to a pharmacist or doctor if:
you have any side effects that bother you or do not go away
They may be able to give you advice about how you can reduce the side effects. Keep taking your medicine unless you're advised to stop.

Serious side effects
Ketoconazole can sometimes cause serious side effects, although most of them are rare.
Possible serious side effects of ketoconazole cream and shampoo include:
an allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) – causing symptoms such as a swollen throat or tongue, a raised, itchy rash and difficulty breathing
a severe skin reaction – causing a rash, peeling skin, blisters and sores
Immediate action required: Call 999 if:
you're using ketoconazole cream or shampoo and think you're having symptoms of a severe skin reaction or anaphylaxis
If you think you're having any other serious side effect, call NHS 111.

Who can and cannot use ketoconazole cream and shampoo
Most adults can use ketoconazole cream and shampoo. Children aged 12 years and over can use ketoconazole shampoo.
But ketoconazole cream and shampoo are not suitable for some people. For example, they may not be suitable if:
you've had an allergic reaction or serious side effect from ketoconazole
Check the packet or leaflet that comes with your medicine to see if it's suitable for you. Speak to a pharmacist or doctor if you're not sure if you can take or use it.
Breastfeeding and ketoconazole cream and shampoo
Speak to a pharmacist or doctor if you're breastfeeding. You can usually use ketoconazole cream or shampoo, but you may need to avoid using it on your breasts or chest.
Pregnancy and ketoconazole cream and shampoo
Ketoconazole cream and shampoo can usually be used be used if you're pregnant or trying to get pregnant, but it's best to check with a doctor or pharmacist.
Using ketoconazole cream and shampoo with other medicines, food and drink
There are some medicines, remedies and supplements that may not mix well with ketoconazole cream and shampoo.
Check the packet or leaflet that comes with your medicine to see if it's suitable for you. Speak to a pharmacist or doctor if you're not sure you can take it.
Food, drink and alcohol with ketoconazole cream and shampoo
You can eat and drink as normal while using ketoconazole cream and shampoo.
