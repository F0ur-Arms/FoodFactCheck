At a glance
Parents play a crucial role in supporting their children’s health and learning at school.
When parents are engaged in their children’s school activities, their children get better grades, choose healthier behaviors, and have better social skills.
Why it's important
Parent engagement in schools
Research shows that parent engagement in schools is closely linked to better student behavior, higher academic achievement, and enhanced social skills.
Parent Engagement in Schools
Engagement involves parents and educators supporting students' learning, development, and health.
