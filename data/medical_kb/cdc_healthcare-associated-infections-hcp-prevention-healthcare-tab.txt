About
The Targeted Assessment for Burnout (TAB) may be used by public health partners or individual healthcare facilities at any time to identify potential factors which may contribute to burnout.
Various personnel in a healthcare facility should have the opportunity to complete the assessment tool.
The facility can use the corresponding implementation guide to address assessment findings.
Technical assistance for assessment collection and summarization is available, upon request.
