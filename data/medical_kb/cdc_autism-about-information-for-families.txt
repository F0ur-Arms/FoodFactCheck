Developmental milestone tools for families
Free "Learn the Signs. Act Early." Materials
CDC's "Learn the Signs. Act Early." program provides free resources and materials for families including information on developmental milestones by age (2 months to 5 years), developmental screening, and a growth chart.
Concerned about your child's development? — Act Early!
If you think your child might have an ASD or you think there could be a problem with the way your child plays, learns, speaks, or acts, contact your child's doctor as soon as possible, and share your concerns. Don't wait. Acting early can make a real difference!
